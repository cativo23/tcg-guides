How it takes Prizes

The deck's plan in four steps.

  1. 1

    Bench

    Poffin puts two Dreepy on the Bench on turn 1. Attach Fire or Psychic to the one that evolves first.

  2. 2

    Dig

    Drakloak on turn 2. Recon Directive looks at the top 2 cards and keeps 1, once for each Drakloak.

  3. 3

    Power

    Crispin attaches one Basic Energy and hands you a second type. Attach that too: Fire + Psychic in one turn.

  4. 4

    200 + 6

    Turn 3 at the earliest: Phantom Dive does 200 to the Active and places 6 counters across their Bench.

How it plays

Opening

  • Keep any hand with a Basic. Best: Dreepy plus Buddy-Buddy Poffin or Poké Pad, plus Lillie's or Crispin.
  • What finds what: Poffin gets Dreepy, Budew and Dunsparce (70 HP or less). Poké Pad gets anything without a Rule Box, Drakloak included. Only Ultra Ball searches the deck for Dragapult ex, Fezandipiti ex and Meowth ex; Night Stretcher gets them back from the discard.
  • Going first: no evolving on turn 1. Bench two or three Dreepy with Poffin and Poké Pad, attach Fire or Psychic to one, and hold Ultra Ball for Dragapult ex.
  • Going second: Items and attachment first, then Lillie's for 8. With Budew Active, Itchy Pollen stops their Poffin, Poké Pad and Ultra Ball next turn.
  • Start Budew or Dunsparce Active. Never Meowth ex: Last-Ditch Catch only works when you play it from your hand to the Bench.
  • Bench order (5 slots): two or three Dreepy → Munkidori with a Darkness Energy → Fezandipiti ex → Meowth ex only on the turn you need its Supporter.

The engine

  • No Rare Candy: Dreepy on turn 1, Drakloak on turn 2, Dragapult ex on turn 3 at the earliest. Keep a second Drakloak with one Energy on the Bench at all times.
  • Phantom Dive costs Fire + Psychic. The list runs 3 of each plus 2 Darkness for Munkidori. Put one Energy on Dreepy or Drakloak a turn early.
  • Crispin finds two Basic Energy of different types: one attached, one to hand. If it finds only one, that one goes to your hand.
  • Lillie's draws 8 until you take your first Prize, then 6. It shuffles your hand away, so play Items and attach first.
  • Dudunsparce draws 3 and shuffles itself back into the deck, so it never gives up a Knock Out. Poké Pad finds both halves.
  • A Benched Dragapult ex takes no damage from attacks (Tera), so the Bench is where the next one gets built. Damage counters still land on it.

Winning

  • Place the 6 counters in this order: Knock Outs now, then a Benched Pokémon left exactly one Phantom Dive from a Knock Out, then Basics about to evolve.
  • Counters to Knock Out: Budew 3, Abra 5, Duskull 6, a 70-HP Basic (Dreepy, N's Zorua, Buneary, Drilbur, Riolu) 7. Non-Darkness Basics benched while your Risky Ruins is in play arrive with 2 already: 70 HP becomes 5.
  • Munkidori with a Darkness Energy moves up to 3 counters from one of your Pokémon to one of theirs, Bench included. It moves nothing unless one of your Pokémon already has counters: their spread, or the 2 your Risky Ruins put on your own Basics.
  • Two-turn Knock Outs on big ex: 6 counters this turn, then Boss's Orders + Phantom Dive (260). Add Adrena-Brain for 290, enough for a 280-HP Zoroark ex. Two Dives (12 counters) + Phantom Dive is 320.
  • Tera stops damage, not counters: Phantom Dive's counters land on a Benched Teal Mask Ogerpon ex or an opposing Dragapult ex. Cruel Arrow's 100 does not.
  • Moltres is the backup attacker: one Fire Energy, 110 against an Active ex.

Decisions

  • Risky Ruins: play it after your own Poffin turns. Its job is putting 2 counters on every non-Darkness Basic they bench; the counters on your own Basics are a side effect Munkidori can move. Their own Stadium (N's Castle, Gravity Mountain) can replace it.
  • Judge or Lillie's: Judge when their hand is big, especially against Alakazam's Powerful Hand (2 counters per card). Lillie's whenever you need cards.
  • Rosa's Encouragement only works while you have more Prizes left than they do. It puts two Basic Energy from the discard on a Stage 2, and Dragapult ex is the only one in the list.
  • Unfair Stamp and Flip the Script need a Knock Out during their last turn. A Knock Out from Poison or Burn during Pokémon Checkup doesn't count, and neither does one on your own turn.
  • Meowth ex and Fezandipiti ex are Pokémon ex: bench them for their Ability, not as walls. Watchtower turns Meowth ex's Ability off.
  • Mirror: Dreepy is Dragon, not Darkness, so a Dreepy benched under your Risky Ruins dies to 5 counters. Whoever thins the other's Dreepy and Drakloak first wins. Spread onto their Benched Dragapult ex: Tera doesn't stop counters.

Combos

  • Crispin + attachment: Fire and Psychic on a Dragapult ex that evolved with no Energy, the same turn.
  • Phantom Dive + Adrena-Brain: 6 + 3 = 9 counters, enough for a Benched Drakloak or Dusclops (90). With two Munkidori, two 70-HP Basics benched under Risky Ruins (5 + 5).
  • Itchy Pollen the turn before they evolve: no Poffin, Poké Pad or Ultra Ball on the turn they need them.
  • After they Knock Out your Pokémon: Unfair Stamp (you draw 5, they draw 2), then Flip the Script for 3 more. Stamp is an Item, so the Supporter is still free.
  • Meowth ex's Tuck Tail does 60 and returns it to your hand; bench it again on a later turn for another Supporter. Only one "Last-Ditch" Ability per turn.

Misplays

  • Planning a turn-2 Dragapult ex. There is no Rare Candy.
  • Playing your own Risky Ruins before Poffin: your Dreepy drop to 50 HP and Budew to 10.
  • Putting counters on Shuppet, Banette, Poltchageist or Sinistcha. Hide 'n' Sneak blocks them, and Adrena-Brain too.
  • Aiming Cruel Arrow or Phantom Dive's 200 at a Benched Tera Pokémon. The damage is prevented.
  • Counting Adrena-Brain with no Darkness Energy on Munkidori, or no counters on your side to move.
  • Using Trading Places as a free retreat. It is Dunsparce's attack and ends your turn.
  • Lillie's or Judge before your Items and attachment.

Tera stops damage from attacks, not damage counters: Phantom Dive's 6 counters land on a Benched Tera Pokémon (Curse Spreading ruling, Scarlet & Violet FAQ, 2023‑03‑30). Mist Energy, Rocky Fighting Energy and Hide 'n' Sneak do block them, and counters aimed there vanish. No ruling names Phantom Dive itself; this follows rulings on the same wording.

Damage routes

Damage to the opponent's Active, before Weakness. Adrena-Brain adds 30 only when Munkidori has a Darkness Energy and one of your Pokémon carries 3 counters to move. The last two rows count counters placed by earlier Phantom Dives.

  1. 10 Budew: Itchy PollenFree. Opponent can't play Items next turn
  2. 40 Dreepy: BiteFire + Psychic
  3. 60 Munkidori: Mind BendPsychic + any. The Active is Confused
  4. 70 Dragapult ex: Jet HeadbuttOne Energy of any type. Drakloak's Dragon Headbutt also does 70
  5. 90 Dudunsparce: Land CrushThree Energy of any type
  6. 100 Fezandipiti ex: Cruel ArrowAny one of their Pokémon. Tera blocks it on the Bench
  7. 110 Moltres: Fighting WingsOne Fire. Against an Active ex; 20 otherwise
  8. 200 Phantom DivePlus 6 counters anywhere on their Bench
  9. 230 Phantom Dive + Adrena-BrainMunkidori with Darkness, 3 counters on your side
  10. 260 Phantom Dive + two Adrena-BrainTwo Munkidori with Darkness, 6 counters on your side
  11. 290 6 counters + Phantom Dive + Adrena-BrainTwo turns. Knocks Out a 280-HP Zoroark ex
  12. 320 12 counters + Phantom DiveThird Dive. Knocks Out a 320-HP Dragapult ex

Matchups

  • Alakazam / Dudunsparce 59.0% 275‑181‑10 Favourable. Phantom Dive knocks out Alakazam (140). Abra has 50 HP, 30 if benched under your Risky Ruins, so one Dive can take two. Powerful Hand places 2 counters per card in their hand: Judge or Unfair Stamp shrinks it, though they still draw for turn and Kadabra (+2) and Alakazam (+3) draw on evolving.
  • Dragapult / Dusknoir 58.8% 287‑195‑6 Favourable. Come and Get You brings back up to 3 Duskull from the discard, so one Knock Out doesn't end the line. Cursed Blast knocks out their own Dusknoir.
  • Dragapult / Blaziken 58.8% 151‑104‑2 Favourable. Their Dreepy line has the same numbers as yours: 7 counters for a Dreepy, 5 under your Ruins. No Blaziken plan checked yet.
  • Alakazam / Dusknoir 58.5% 190‑133‑2 Favourable. No full plan checked yet. Abra and Duskull on the Bench are the Phantom Dive targets before they evolve.
  • Ogerpon / Meganium / Hydrapple 58.4% 173‑119‑4 Favourable. No full plan checked yet. Teal Mask Ogerpon ex's Tera stops Phantom Dive's 200 on the Bench, not its counters.

  • Mega Lucario ex 54.2% 83‑68‑2 Even; small sample. A Benched Riolu (80 or 70 HP; 60 or 50 under your Ruins) dies to the 6 counters before it becomes a Mega. Mega Lucario ex (340) takes two turns: Phantom Dive leaves it at 140, then a second Dive. Keep Risky Ruins over Gravity Mountain, which drops Dragapult ex to 290 so Mega Brave + Premium Power Pro (300) knocks it out. Crushing Hammer is not an attack, so Rocky Fighting Energy doesn't stop it; heads is 50%.
  • Slowking 50.8% 253‑240‑5 Even. Phantom Dive knocks out Slowking (120). A Benched Slowpoke (80, 60 under your Ruins) dies to the counters. Kyurem's Trifrost can't damage a Benched Dragapult ex (Tera), but Annihilape's Ghostly Blow places 5 counters and gets through. Annihilape's Durable Body: a heads keeps it at 10 HP. Save the second Dragapult ex for Mega Kangaskhan ex (300).
  • N's Zoroark 50.4% 277‑258‑15 Even. Lead Budew and Itchy Pollen to slow their Poffin and Poké Pad. Put the first Dive's 6 counters on a Benched Zoroark ex; next turn Boss's + Phantom Dive + Adrena-Brain makes 290 on its 280 HP, or Moltres (110) then Phantom Dive. Zorua is Darkness, so your Ruins skips it. Crushing Hammer their Darkness Energy: Night Joker costs two. Rampaging Thunder + Black Belt's (290) doesn't knock out Dragapult ex; it does with Adrena-Brain (320) or with Binding Mochi on a Poisoned Zoroark ex (330).
  • Lopunny / Dudunsparce 49.8% 143‑141‑3 Even. Mega Lopunny ex (330) needs two hits. Gale Thrust does 230 only on the turn it moved up from the Bench, so it doesn't knock out a 320-HP Dragapult ex either. Buneary, Dunsparce and Fan Rotom have 70 HP: 5 counters each under your Ruins.
  • Dhelmise 46.7% 175‑194‑6 Even. Phantom Dive knocks out Dhelmise (140) in one hit; Vengeful Anchor needs two hits (170 + 170) on Dragapult ex. Put the counters only on Benched Dhelmise: Hide 'n' Sneak blocks them and Adrena-Brain. Count their discard: at 4 Hide 'n' Sneak Pokémon Anchor does 170, at 6 Matcha Spin puts 4 counters on each of your Pokémon. Don't Knock Out a Shuppet or Poltchageist when it would take them to 4 or 6.
  • Sharpedo / Toxtricity 45.8% 119‑140‑1 Even. No plan checked for this guide yet.
  • Basic Box 43.9% 240‑294‑13 Unfavourable. Raging Bolt ex's Bellowing Thunder does 70 per Basic Energy discarded, 350 with five. Mega Kangaskhan ex (300) needs two Dives. Phantom Dive's counters still land on Benched Ogerpon ex. Iron Crown ex's Twin Shotels ignores effects on the Pokémon, so Tera won't stop it.
  • Mega Excadrill ex 43.4% 175‑220‑8 Unfavourable. Maximum Drilling with 2 extra Energy (330) knocks out Dragapult ex, so Crushing Hammer is your key card: each Energy removed takes it back toward 200. Moltres's 110 against an ex doubles to 220 on Fire Weakness, so Moltres plus Phantom Dive takes Mega Excadrill ex (340). Counters on a Benched Drilbur or Beldum (70, 50 under your Ruins) knock them out.
  • Crustle 33.8% 69‑130‑5 Worst matchup. Mysterious Rock Inn: Crustle takes no damage from attacks by Pokémon ex, so Phantom Dive's 200 does nothing to it. Phantom Dive's counters land on a Crustle without Mist Energy, and Adrena-Brain (an Ability) works on any Crustle. Hit Dwebble (70, 50 under your Ruins) on the Bench; non-ex attackers (Drakloak 70, Dudunsparce 90, Moltres) do the damage.

What to buy

Starting from a collection that already holds the N's Zoroark ex deck. Prices are per-card TCGplayer prices Limitless showed on 2026‑10‑14, times the copies needed: about US$29.83 for 25 cards. Local Spanish copies will differ. Basic Energy is not counted.

  1. 1

    4 Dreepy, 4 Drakloak, 3 Dragapult ex US$5.01

    The whole engine, and the cheapest step: Dragapult ex is about US$1.15 a copy.

  2. 2

    Dunsparce, Dudunsparce, Fezandipiti ex, Meowth ex, Moltres PFL 14 (1 each) US$9.37

    Support Pokémon the collection doesn't have. The spare Budew covers one slot; two Munkidori and the other Budew move over from the Zoroark deck. The Moltres in the collection (30th Celebration #130) is a different card.

  3. 3

    2 Crispin, Rosa's Encouragement, 3 Crushing Hammer, 2 Risky Ruins US$1.55

    Cheap Trainers. The third Night Stretcher, one Ultra Ball and one Crushing Hammer are already spare in the collection. Add 3 Psychic, 3 Fire and 2 Darkness Energy.

  4. 4

    Unfair Stamp · TWM 165 US$13.90

    Almost half the total. All 32 top-32 Dragapult ex lists play one. Until you have it, a fourth Ultra Ball (US$0.21) fills the slot; 5 of those 32 lists play four.

  5. 5

    Second copies: Budew, 2 Munkidori, Lillie's, 3 Boss's Orders, Judge, 4 Poffin, 4 Poké Pad, 2 Night Stretcher, 2 Ultra Ball US$5.13

    Only if both decks stay sleeved at once. Otherwise move these 20 cards over from the Zoroark deck.
