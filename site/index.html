How it plays

Opening

  • Keep any hand with a Basic. Best: N's Zorua plus Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Ultra Ball or Poké Pad.
  • Going first: no Supporter, no attack — just bench. Poffin, Poké Pad, Ultra Ball, as many Zorua as you can, Pecharunt ex if you have it.
  • Going second: Supporter right away — Cyrano for Zoroark ex, or Lillie's Determination if the hand missed. Budew Active can Itchy Pollen for free.
  • Bench order (5 slots): two Zorua → Budew → Pecharunt ex → Zekrom / Reshiram as Night Joker sources → Munkidori only if you can spare the attachment.

The engine

  • Trade every turn with every Zoroark ex in play. Discard spare Darkness Energy first — N's PP Up and Night Stretcher bring it back.
  • Don't discard the single Black Belt's Training, Janine's Secret Art, Secret Box or Dark Bell early.
  • Lillie's draws 8 while you still have all 6 Prize cards. Play Items and attach first — the rest of your hand gets shuffled away.
  • One Supporter a turn is the real decision: Lillie's, Cyrano, Boss's Orders, Janine's or Black Belt's.
  • Trade → discard → N's PP Up powers Night Joker without spending your Supporter.

Winning

  • Take Prizes with Night Joker. Virtuous Flame (170, no drawback) is the default; save Rampaging Thunder and the +40 cards for 2-Prize ex.
  • Only Zoroark ex and Pecharunt ex give up 2 Prizes. Keep Zekrom and Reshiram benched and a second Zoroark ex loaded.
  • Boss's Orders (×4) sets the tempo: pull up something already damaged, or a 2-Prize ex a boosted Rampaging Thunder takes in one hit.
  • Pecharunt ex is the comeback attacker — 240 at 4 Prizes taken, 300 at 5.

Combos

  • Chains + Mochi + Black Belt's: Mochi on a Benched Zoroark ex with DD → Subjugating Chains swaps it Active and Poisons it (an Ability, so the Supporter is free) → Black Belt's → Rampaging Thunder for 330.
  • Janine's route: Janine's on the Active Zoroark ex Poisons it and turns on Mochi — but uses the Supporter.
  • Recycle poison: Munkidori moves up to 3 poison counters off your Zoroark ex onto theirs.
  • Dark Bell + Clutch: Yveltal stops the retreat, so the only way out of Confusion is a switch card.

Misplays

  • Copying Rampaging Thunder with no backup attacker ready.
  • N's PP Up on Munkidori, Pecharunt ex, Budew or Yveltal — it only reaches Benched N's Pokémon.
  • Chains or Janine's on Zekrom / Reshiram — they're Dragon type, so Mochi never turns on.
  • Black Belt's Training against a non-ex.
  • Dark Bell with Zekrom, Reshiram, Munkidori or Budew Active — they get Confused too.
  • Leaving Zoroark ex Active against Grass attackers.

When Zoroark ex copies an attack, Zoroark ex is the attacker — so Rampaging Thunder's "can't attack next turn" lands on it. Standard copy-attack ruling; the card text doesn't spell it out.

Damage routes

Damage to the opponent's Active, before Weakness. Munkidori's Adrena-Brain adds 30 on top of any line; Pecharunt ex's Irritated Outburst scales with Prizes taken (180 / 240 / 300).
