The deck's engine in four steps: draw, reload, poison, hit.

    Trade

    Discard a spare Darkness Energy, draw 2. Once per turn for each Zoroark ex in play.

  2. 2

    Reload

    N's PP Up puts that Energy back on a Benched Zoroark ex, and your Supporter stays free.

  3. 3

    Poison

    Subjugating Chains swaps the Mochi-holding Zoroark ex in and Poisons it: +40.

  4. 4

    330

    Black Belt's Training (+40), then Night Joker → Rampaging Thunder (250) on an ex.

How it plays

Opening

  • Keep any hand with a Basic. Best: N's Zorua plus Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Ultra Ball or Poké Pad.
  • What finds what: Poffin gets Zorua and Budew (70 HP or less). Poké Pad gets anything without a Rule Box. Only Ultra Ball and Cyrano find Zoroark ex and Pecharunt ex.
  • Going first: no Supporter, no attack and no evolving on turn 1. Bench with Poffin, Poké Pad and Ultra Ball, and attach your Energy to a Benched Zorua.
  • Going second: play a Supporter right away. Cyrano if two Zorua are already down, so both Zoroark ex are in hand for turn 2; Lillie's for 8 if you're missing Zorua. Budew Active can Itchy Pollen.
  • Bench order (5 slots): two Zorua → Zekrom or Reshiram → Pecharunt ex → Munkidori or Budew. Skip Budew against Dragapult: 30 HP dies to spread.

The engine

  • Trade every turn with every Zoroark ex in play. Discard spare Darkness Energy first, since N's PP Up and Night Stretcher bring it back.
  • Don't discard the single Black Belt's Training, Janine's Secret Art or Prime Catcher early.
  • Lillie's draws 8 while you still have all 6 Prize cards. Play Items and attach first, because the rest of your hand gets shuffled away.
  • No Energy on Zekrom or Reshiram. Night Joker pays DD from Zoroark ex; the source only has to sit on the Bench.

Winning

  • Take Prizes with Night Joker. Virtuous Flame (170, no drawback) is the default; save Rampaging Thunder and the +40 cards for 2-Prize ex.
  • Only Zoroark ex and Pecharunt ex give up 2 Prizes. Keep Zekrom and Reshiram benched and a second Zoroark ex loaded.
  • Boss's Orders and Prime Catcher set the tempo: pull up something already damaged, an evolving Basic, or a 2-Prize ex a boosted Rampaging Thunder takes in one hit.
  • Pecharunt ex is the comeback attacker: Irritated Outburst does 60 for each Prize your opponent has taken, so 240 once they've taken 4.
  • Cheapest Prizes to give up: Budew, then Yveltal or a spare Zorua. Never a powered Zoroark ex.

Decisions

  • Prime Catcher or Boss's: Prime Catcher when this turn's Supporter has to be Black Belt's or Lillie's, or when you also need to switch your own Active. Boss's when the Supporter is free.
  • Judge: right after they take a Knock Out, or when they hold 6+ cards and you hold few. Items and attach first, then Judge, then Trade with every Zoroark ex. Not on turn 1 or 2 (Lillie's draws more), and not when your hand already has the KO.
  • Ahead, facing disruption: put Mochi and Energy on the board before your Supporter. Cards in play can't be shuffled away, and Trade refills the hand.
  • Stuck Active: Castle makes N's Pokémon retreat for free, and moving to the Bench clears Poison and Confusion. Pecharunt ex and Munkidori aren't N's Pokémon; use Chains or Prime Catcher instead.

Combos

  • Chains + Mochi + Black Belt's: Mochi on a Benched Zoroark ex with DD → Subjugating Chains swaps it Active and Poisons it (an Ability, so the Supporter is free) → Black Belt's → Rampaging Thunder for 330.
  • Prime Catcher + Black Belt's: Prime Catcher is an Item, so it pulls up their ex and switches your powered Zoroark ex Active without spending the Supporter. Play Black Belt's Training, then Rampaging Thunder for 290.
  • Janine's route: Janine's on the Active Zoroark ex Poisons it and turns on Mochi, but it uses your Supporter.
  • Recycle damage: Munkidori moves up to 3 damage counters off one of your Pokémon (poison, or their spread) onto theirs: +30 to any line.

Misplays

  • Copying Rampaging Thunder with no follow-up: have a second powered Zoroark ex ready.
  • N's PP Up on Munkidori, Pecharunt ex, Budew or Yveltal. It only reaches Benched N's Pokémon.
  • Counting Munkidori as an attacker. Mind Bend needs Psychic Energy and the list has none.
  • Black Belt's Training against a non-ex.
  • Leaving Zoroark ex Active against Grass attackers.

When Zoroark ex copies an attack, Zoroark ex is the attacker, so Rampaging Thunder's "can't attack next turn" lands on it. Standard copy-attack ruling; the card text doesn't spell it out. Rulings say attack effects on the Defending Pokémon end when it moves to the Bench, but none covers the attacker yet, so ask your judge before retreating it to attack again.

Damage routes

Damage to the opponent's Active, before Weakness. Munkidori's Adrena-Brain adds 30 on top of any line, but only when one of your Pokémon already has damage counters to move: 290 becomes 320 for Dragapult ex once their spread has hit your Bench. Pecharunt ex's Irritated Outburst scales with Prizes your opponent has taken (180 / 240 / 300).

Matchups

N's Zoroark's win rate in 30th Celebration (Limitless, 2026‑10‑06). Online games with every Zoroark variant mixed together. Left of the line loses, right of it wins. These numbers move week to week.

  • Dhelmise 67.1% 104‑50 Dhelmise is weak to Darkness: Night Joker → Shred does 140 and KOs it. Save Rampaging Thunder and Black Belt's for their ex. Every Shuppet you KO feeds Vengeful Anchor, so Boss's the Dhelmise instead.
  • Grimmsnarl Froslass 64.6% 31‑17 Small sample. Froslass puts a damage counter on every Pokémon with an Ability each Checkup, which gives Adrena-Brain fuel. Virtuous Flame KOs Froslass.
  • Slowking 51.9% 109‑98 Slowking is weak to Darkness: Shred (140) KOs it. Save Rampaging Thunder for Kangaskhan.
  • Dragapult / Blaziken 50.0% 49‑45 Even. Same plan as vs. Dragapult ex.
  • Dragapult / Dusknoir 49.3% 105‑107 Even. Phantom Dive + Cursed Blast (330) does KO a Zoroark ex. Shred KOs Duskull and Dusclops (weak to Darkness) before they use their Ability; Virtuous Flame KOs Dusknoir.
  • N's Zoroark (mirror) 48.2% 105‑105 Even. Rampaging Thunder + Black Belt's or Mochi (290) KOs their 280-HP Zoroark ex. Their Shred KOs your Munkidori.
  • Basic Box 47.7% 103‑109 Meowth ex (170) falls to Virtuous Flame. Lillie's Clefairy ex makes your Zekrom and Reshiram weak to Psychic, so don't bench them before you need them.
  • Dragapult ex 46.2% 190‑211 Lead Budew and never bench it: their spread KOs it. Pull up Dreepy or Drakloak before they evolve. Under Risky Ruins, bench Darkness Pokémon first and cover the Stadium with Castle before Zekrom or Munkidori. On a 320-HP Dragapult ex: Rampaging Thunder + Black Belt's + Mochi (330), with a second Zoroark ex powered.
  • Alakazam / Dudunsparce 42.7% 79‑102 Abra, Kadabra and Alakazam are weak to Darkness: Shred does 140. Judge shrinks the hand that powers their damage.
  • Mega Excadrill ex 42.5% 71‑94 Maximum Drilling can reach 330 and OHKO Zoroark ex. Take out Drilbur before it powers up, and plan two-hit KOs on Excadrill; Boss's Orders for easier Prizes.
  • Mega Lucario ex 36.9% 24‑40 Small sample. Mega Brave + Premium Power Pro (300) KOs a Zoroark ex, but a Mega gives up 3 Prizes: two KOs win. Lead Budew going second so Itchy Pollen stops Premium Power Pro on their turn 2, and pull up Riolu (80 HP). The turn after a Mega Brave, hit it with Virtuous Flame + Black Belt's (210): it can't Mega Brave again, and Aura Jab (130) doesn't KO a healthy Zoroark ex. Finish with Virtuous Flame (380 total), and Boss's it back if they retreat it. Keep Pecharunt ex off the Bench (Fighting weakness).
  • Ogerpon / Meganium / Hydrapple 31.8% 34‑72 Grass attackers into Zoroark ex's Grass weakness. No tested plan yet.
  • Festival Lead 25.0% 21‑63 Worst matchup. Festival Lead only attacks twice while Festival Grounds is in play: cover it with N's Castle every turn. Virtuous Flame KOs their Pokémon; take Thwackey first.

Upgrades

Make these swaps in order; each one leaves a legal 60. Counts are from 119 N's Zoroark lists: 71 from Brisbane, Frankfurt and Recife (30th Celebration, 2026‑09‑26 to 10‑03) and 48 earlier ones. Small samples: a direction, not proof.

  1. Out

    →
    In
    2 Team Rocket's Watchtower · DRI 180

    Colorless Pokémon in play have no Abilities, so Trade keeps working while their Dudunsparce, Kangaskhan and Meowth ex switch off. Four Stadiums also win the fight against Risky Ruins, Jamming Tower and Festival Grounds. In 94 of 119 lists; 108 of 119 play exactly 3 PP Up.

  2. Out
    2nd Judge
    →
    In
    Special Red Card · CRI 82

    Once your opponent has 3 or fewer Prizes left, their hand goes to the bottom of the deck and they draw 3. It's an Item, so your Supporter stays free for Black Belt's on the closing turns. In 117 of 119 lists.

  3. Out
    3rd Ultra Ball
    →
    In
    Fezandipiti ex · ASC 142 or SFA 38

    Flip the Script draws 3 after one of your Pokémon is Knocked Out, and it's a third Cyrano target. In 110 of 119 lists. Fighting weakness: keep it off the Bench against Lucario.

  4. Out
    4th Boss's Orders
    →
    In
    Tatsugiri · TWM 131

    Attract Customers: while Active, once a turn look at the top 6 cards and take a Supporter. 70 HP, so Poffin and Poké Pad find it; Dragon type, so Watchtower doesn't switch it off. Prime Catcher keeps you at 4 gust cards. In 12 of the 19 top‑64 lists.

  5. Out
    Prime Catcher
    →
    In
    Secret Box · TWM 163

    Discard 3 to search an Item, a Tool, a Supporter and a Stadium: Mochi, Black Belt's and a Stadium from one card. The data doesn't favour it over Unfair Stamp (68% of top‑64 lists play it against 81% of the rest); it's here because it's cheaper and easier to find. Until you have one, Prime Catcher stays.
