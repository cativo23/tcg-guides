01 — Format

Standard legality

2026 rotation confirmed: only regulation marks H, I, and J are legal in Standard right now (digital since March 26, 2026; paper since April 10, 2026). Every card in this list falls inside that window — nothing here rotates out before you finish building it.

Check your own binder: if you already own cards from older sets (30th Celebration, Mysterious Treasures, Paldea Evolved reg. G, Silver Tempest reg. F), those are no longer Standard-legal — they're Unlimited/casual only now.
02 — Selection

Why this deck

N's Zoroark is the 5th most-played archetype in Standard after Pitch Black (5.42% meta share), and it put two players in the Top 10 at Worlds 2026. Its strength is consistency rather than one big attacker.

Zoroark ex's Ability Trade — "You must discard a card from your hand in order to use this Ability. Once during your turn, you may draw 2 cards" — stacks: with three Zoroark ex in play that's three Trades a turn. Its attack, Night Joker (DD), uses an attack from one of your Benched N's Pokémon, which is how this list reaches N's Zekrom's 250 and N's Reshiram's 170 without running a single Fire or Lightning Energy.

This build leans into that consistency with four each of Ultra Ball, Poké Pad and Boss's Orders, and adds a poison package — Pecharunt ex, Janine's Secret Art, Binding Mochi, two Munkidori and Dark Bell — for the extra damage that turns big hits into one-hit KOs on Pokémon ex.

04 — How to play it

Strategy

Opening

  • Keep any hand with a Basic. The best keep has N's Zorua plus Buddy-Buddy Poffin, Ultra Ball or Poké Pad; the weakest is non-N's Basics (Munkidori, Pecharunt ex, Yveltal) with no Zorua and no search.
  • Buddy-Buddy Poffin only finds N's Zorua (70 HP) and Budew (30 HP) — every other Basic is over the 70-HP cap. Poké Pad finds any non-Rule-Box Pokémon, so not Zoroark ex or Pecharunt ex. Ultra Ball finds anything; Cyrano finds Zoroark ex and Pecharunt ex.
  • Going first: no Supporter and no attack on turn 1 — just bench: Poffin, Poké Pad, Ultra Ball, as many Zorua as you can, Pecharunt ex if you have it.
  • Going second: play a Supporter right away (Cyrano for Zoroark ex, or Lillie's Determination if the hand missed). With Budew Active, Itchy Pollen is free and stops your opponent's Items next turn.
  • Bench priority (5 slots): two N's Zorua → Budew if Poffin brought it → Pecharunt ex → N's Zekrom / N's Reshiram as Night Joker sources → Munkidori only if you can spare the Darkness attachment.

The engine

  • Trade every turn, with every Zoroark ex in play. Best discards: spare Darkness Energy (N's PP Up and Night Stretcher bring it back), extra Zorua once the bench is set, spent situational cards, late-game Budew.
  • Don't discard the single Black Belt's Training, Janine's Secret Art, Secret Box or Dark Bell early, or your last Cyrano before the ex line is found. Night Stretcher returns Pokémon or Basic Energy — not Trainers.
  • Lillie's Determination draws 6, or 8 while you still have all 6 Prize cards — strongest before your first KO. Play Items and attach Energy first; everything left in hand gets shuffled away.
  • One Supporter per turn is the real decision: Lillie's (refuel), Cyrano (ex bodies), Boss's Orders (target), Janine's Secret Art (energy) or Black Belt's Training (+40 vs. an ex).
  • N's PP Up attaches a Basic Energy from the discard to a Benched N's Pokémon (Zorua, Zoroark ex, Zekrom, Reshiram) — the Trade → discard → PP Up loop powers Night Joker without spending your Supporter.
  • Janine's Secret Art attaches a Darkness Energy from the deck to up to 2 Darkness Pokémon (Zorua, Zoroark ex, Pecharunt ex, Yveltal). If one is your Active, it's Poisoned — which turns on Binding Mochi.
  • Secret Box (discard 3) always grabs Binding Mochi and N's Castle, plus the Item and Supporter of your choice — e.g. N's PP Up + Black Belt's Training for a planned knockout turn.
  • N's Castle removes the Retreat Cost of all N's Pokémon — on both sides, so be careful in the mirror.

Win condition

  • Take Prizes with Night Joker from a Zoroark ex with DD. Virtuous Flame (170, no drawback) is the default; save Rampaging Thunder and the +40 cards for 2-Prize ex, where three KOs win.
  • Only Zoroark ex and Pecharunt ex give up 2 Prizes. Keep Zekrom and Reshiram on the Bench as 1-Prize attack sources, and have a second Zoroark ex loaded so a locked or KO'd attacker is replaced immediately.
  • Boss's Orders (×4) sets the tempo: pull up something already damaged by Adrena-Brain or poison, or a 2-Prize ex that a boosted Rampaging Thunder takes in one hit.
  • Pecharunt ex is the comeback attacker — Irritated Outburst gets stronger the further behind you are (240 at 4 Prizes taken, 300 at 5).

Key combos

  • Poison + Mochi + Black Belt's in one turn: Binding Mochi on a Benched Zoroark ex with DD → Pecharunt ex's Subjugating Chains switches it Active and Poisons it (an Ability, so your Supporter is still free) → Black Belt's Training → Rampaging Thunder for 330 on an ex, or Virtuous Flame for 250 with no lock.
  • Janine's route: Janine's Secret Art on the Active Zoroark ex + one Benched Darkness Pokémon. The Active is Poisoned and Mochi turns on — but Janine's uses the Supporter, so no Black Belt's that turn.
  • Recycle poison damage: poison puts counters on your own Zoroark ex between turns; Munkidori's Adrena-Brain moves up to 3 of them onto an opposing Pokémon — healing your attacker and adding 30 toward a KO.
  • Yveltal's Clutch (D, 20) stops the Defending Pokémon from retreating next turn, holding a damaged target in place.
  • Dark Bell confuses both non-Darkness Active Pokémon. Play it with Zoroark ex, Zorua or Yveltal Active — not Zekrom, Reshiram, Munkidori or Budew.

Common misplays

  • Copying Rampaging Thunder with no backup attacker ready — that Zoroark ex can't attack next turn.
  • N's PP Up on Munkidori, Pecharunt ex, Budew or Yveltal — it only targets Benched N's Pokémon.
  • Subjugating Chains or Janine's on Zekrom/Reshiram — they're Dragon type, so they can't be Poisoned this way and Mochi never turns on.
  • Black Belt's Training against a non-ex, where it adds nothing.
  • Lillie's Determination with playable Items or an unused Energy attachment still in hand.
  • Munkidori as an attacker — Mind Bend needs Psychic Energy, which the list doesn't run. It's a Bench Adrena-Brain piece.
  • Leaving Zoroark ex Active against Grass attackers — it's weak to Grass.
Copied attacks: when Zoroark ex uses a copied attack, Zoroark ex is the attacker — so Rampaging Thunder's "can't attack next turn" and Powerful Rage's damage count apply to Zoroark ex. That follows standard copy-attack rulings; the card text itself doesn't spell it out.
